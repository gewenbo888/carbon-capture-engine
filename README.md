# Carbon Capture Engine · 碳捕获引擎

A civilization-scale, bilingual (EN · 中文) exploration of carbon — not as pollution but as the bookkeeping of a living planet. From photosynthesis and the geochemical cycle to industrial combustion, the greenhouse effect, direct air capture, biological and oceanic sinks, carbon-negative energy, geoengineering, AI-coordinated Earth systems, and the terraforming of dead worlds — the platform argues that carbon capture may be the opening move in civilization learning to consciously regulate its own planetary metabolism.

Ten interactive systems, a live planetary carbon-cycle simulation, an 8-term Planetary Carbon Stability model, an 11-layer recursive carbon engine, and a six-lens AI Carbon Analyst — all switching between English and Chinese without reload.

## Systems

1. The Origin of the Carbon Cycle — Earth as a slow-turning engine moving carbon between sky, sea, life and stone
2. Industrialization & Atmospheric Transformation — burning the buried past faster than the planet can rebury it
3. The Greenhouse Effect & Climate Dynamics — energy balance, feedbacks, tipping points
4. Direct Air Capture & Industrial Carbon Removal — machines that run the cycle backward
5. Biological & Oceanic Carbon Systems — forests, soil, the ocean's biological pump
6. Energy Systems & Carbon-Negative Economies — decarbonizing the bloodstream of civilization
7. Geoengineering & Planetary Management — the temptation to turn down the sun
8. AI, Climate Modeling & Autonomous Ecological Systems — a planetary nervous system
9. Space, Terraforming & Future Biospheres — carbon management as the founding craft of off-world life
10. The Unified Planetary Carbon Model — one framework for the leaf, the smokestack, and the managed planet
